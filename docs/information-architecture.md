# TEACHER OS — Information Architecture & Navigation Taxonomy

---

## 1. Teacher Ecosystem Navigation

```
TEACHER APP ROOT
│
├── 🏠 HOME: "MY INTELLIGENT DAY" (يومي الذكي)
│   ├── Greeting & Today's Schedule Timeline
│   ├── Top Actionable Priority Card (e.g. "Review Ohm's Law with Group 1")
│   ├── High-Risk / Needy Students Spotlight
│   ├── Quick Action Bar (New Quiz, Mark Attendance, Voice Command)
│   └── Business Snapshot (Today's Revenue, Expiring Subscriptions)
│
├── 👥 STUDENTS & GROUPS (الطلاب والمجموعات)
│   ├── Groups Directory (Group Capacity, Timetable, Fee Status)
│   ├── Group Detail (Roster, Sessions, Attendance Heatmap, Performance)
│   ├── Student Intelligence Profile (Attendance, Attempts, Concept Mastery, Notes)
│   └── Add / Import Students (Bulk Paste, Excel/CSV, QR Invite)
│
├── 🤖 AI STUDIO (استوديو الذكاء الاصطناعي)
│   ├── AI Lesson Builder (Curriculum Objectives, Hooks, Structure, Time Allocation)
│   ├── Assessment Generator (MCQ, True/False, Short Answer, Difficulty Matrix)
│   ├── Homework & Revision Generator (Targeted Micro-Interventions)
│   ├── Parent Communication Drafter (Polite Egyptian Arabic Announcements)
│   └── Knowledge Vault Search (Semantic RAG over uploaded teacher materials)
│
├── 📊 INSIGHTS & GAPS (تحليل الأداء والفجوات)
│   ├── Group Concept Mastery Matrix (Green / Amber / Red)
│   ├── High-Frequency Mistake Mining
│   ├── Attendance vs Performance Correlation
│   └── Closed-Loop Remediation Suggestions
│
├── 💼 BUSINESS OS (إدارة العمل والاشتراكات)
│   ├── Monthly Revenue & Expense Tracking
│   ├── Subscription Expirations & Overdue Payments
│   ├── Assistant Permissions & Audit Logs
│   └── Export Reports (PDF / Excel / WhatsApp Cards)
│
└── ⚙️ SETTINGS & PROFILE (الإعدادات)
    ├── Teacher Digital Identity (Subjects, Grades, Preferred Tone)
    ├── Localization (Arabic Egyptian / English / RTL)
    └── Security, Privacy & Export Data
```

## 2. Student Ecosystem Navigation

```
STUDENT APP ROOT
│
├── 📅 TODAY (اليوم)
│   ├── Next Class Countdown & Room/Center Info
│   ├── Active Assignments & Quizzes with Deadlines
│   └── Daily Micro-Revision Challenge
│
├── 📝 ASSESSMENTS & TASKS (الواجبات والاختبارات)
│   ├── Available Quizzes
│   ├── Past Attempts & Detailed Explanations
│   └── Step-by-Step Problem Solving Worksheets
│
├── 📈 MY PROGRESS (مستواي التعليمي)
│   ├── Concept Mastery Radar (Strong vs Needs Practice)
│   ├── Attendance Streak & Points
│   └── Teacher Feedback & Observations
│
└── 💬 AI STUDY COACH (المرشد التعليمي الذكي)
    ├── Socratic Question & Hint Mode (Context-Bounded to Class Topics)
    └── Revision Flashcards
```

## 3. Parent Ecosystem Navigation

```
PARENT APP ROOT
│
├── 💓 CHILD PULSE (نبض الأبناء)
│   ├── Child Selector (Multi-Child Support)
│   ├── Overall Status: [Good | Needs Attention | Action Recommended]
│   ├── Attendance Indicator (Recent Sessions Attended / Absent)
│   └── Homework & Quiz Score Stream
│
├── 📢 ANNOUNCEMENTS & NOTES (تنبيهات المعلم)
│   ├── Teacher Announcements & Exam Schedules
│   └── Direct Teacher Feedback Cards
│
└── 💳 PAYMENTS & RECEIPTS (المدفوعات والإيصالات)
    ├── Monthly Subscription Due Dates
    ├── Instant Payment Confirmation (InstaPay / Fawry / Cash)
    └── Downloadable Payment History
```

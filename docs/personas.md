## 1. Primary Persona: The High-Volume Private Tutor

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Persona: Mr. Tarek El-Shennawy (أ/ طارق الشناوي)                        │
│ Role: Secondary School Physics Tutor (مدرس فيزياء ثانوية عامة)        │
│ Age: 38 | Location: Nasr City & Heliopolis, Cairo, Egypt              │
│ Workload: 14 Groups (650+ students across 3 centers) + 1-on-1 sessions │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Background & Context
Mr. Tarek is a popular, ambitious private tutor. He charges per session or monthly subscription. He employs two university students as teaching assistants to manage paper attendance, collect cash/InstaPay, and grade weekly paper homework.

### Key Pain Points
1. **Assessment Creation Grind**: Spends 6+ hours every weekend typing up multiple quiz versions in MS Word to prevent students from sharing answers across morning and evening groups.
2. **Attendance & Fee Leakage**: Assistants frequently misplace paper attendance sheets, leading to uncollected fees and awkward disputes with parents.
3. **Parent Inquiries Overload**: Receives dozens of WhatsApp messages every evening asking: *"How is Mohamed doing? Did he attend today? Why did he get 12/20 on the quiz?"*
4. **Lack of Remedial Precision**: Knows intuitively that his students struggle with electrical circuits, but lacks exact data on whether the issue is conceptual physics or basic algebraic calculation.

### Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)
- *When* I am preparing for my next round of sessions, *I want to* generate a rigorous, curriculum-aligned assessment with balanced difficulty in 2 minutes, *so that* I can evaluate student understanding without sacrificing my weekend.
- *When* a student finishes an assessment, *I want to* know the exact cognitive concepts they failed, *so that* I can deliver a 5-minute targeted explanation in the next class.
- *When* parents want updates, *I want* professional, automated progress summaries sent with 1 tap, *so that* I maintain trust without spending hours on WhatsApp.

## 2. Secondary Persona: The High-Stakes Student

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Persona: Salma Mahmoud (سلمى محمود)                                    │
│ Role: 3rd Secondary Student (طالبة ثانوية عامة - علمي علوم)            │
│ Age: 17 | Location: Alexandria, Egypt                                  │
│ Workload: 7 Subjects with different private tutors                    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Background & Context
Salma is studying under intense academic pressure for her high school completion exams. She commutes between tutoring centers and spends late nights studying across disparate physical notebooks, PDF summaries, and online groups.

### Key Pain Points
1. **Scattered Homework & Deadlines**: Forgets which tutor assigned which worksheet.
2. **No Immediate Feedback**: When stuck on a hard problem at 11:30 PM, she has no access to her teacher and resorts to memorizing answer keys.
3. **Anxiety & Lack of Clarity**: Has no structured view of her mastery gaps; feels overwhelmed without knowing where to focus her revision hours.

### Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)
- *When* I sit down to study, *I want to* see all my upcoming tasks, quizzes, and weak topics in one unified daily view, *so that* I use my limited study time efficiently.
- *When* I make a mistake on a quiz question, *I want* a step-by-step Socratic explanation that guides me through the underlying concept, *so that* I don't repeat the mistake on the final exam.

## 3. Tertiary Persona: The Anxious, Invested Parent

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Persona: Hajj Mahmoud Ibrahim (الحاج محمود إبراهيم)                     │
│ Role: Father & Business Owner (ولي أمر طالبين)                         │
│ Age: 52 | Location: Giza, Egypt                                        │
│ Context: Spends 25%+ of household income on private tutoring          │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Background & Context
Hajj Mahmoud works long hours and wants to ensure his children are attending their lessons, doing their homework, and on track to enter top universities. He is intimidated by complex software and uses WhatsApp as his primary mobile communication tool.

### Key Pain Points
1. **Uncertainty**: Never sure if his son actually arrived at the tutoring center or skipped the session.
2. **Delayed Warnings**: Only finds out a child has been failing quizzes when it's too late in the academic term.
3. **Payment Tracking Friction**: Wants clear receipts for monthly tutor fees without keeping crumpled paper slips.

### Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)
- *When* my child attends or misses a private lesson, *I want* an instant, clear notification, *so that* I know they are safe and disciplined.
- *When* I check my child's progress, *I want* a simple, clear status ("Child Pulse") and clear guidance on how to support them, *so that* I am confident in their academic trajectory.

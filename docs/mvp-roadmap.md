## 2. North Star Metric & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Primary North Star Metric:
$$\text{Closed Teaching Loops Completed per Active Teacher per Week} \ge 2.5$$
*(A complete loop: Plan/Teach $\rightarrow$ Assess $\rightarrow$ Mine Gap $\rightarrow$ Apply Intervention).*

### Secondary Activation Metrics:
- **Time-to-First-Assessment**: $< 10\text{ minutes}$ from registration.
- **Weekly Teacher Retention**: $> 75\%$ in Month 3.
- **Parent Engagement Rate**: $> 80\%$ weekly check-ins on Child Pulse.
